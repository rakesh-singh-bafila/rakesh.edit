How The Magic Happens

A systematic 4-step pipeline designed to turn raw ideas into high-retention video content.

01

Ingest & Hook Selection

Raw footage organization, audio cleaning, and extracting the most compelling 3-second hook to stop the scroll instantly.

02

High-Retention Pacing

Trimming filler, applying speed ramps, beat-matching audio, and engineering seamless narrative flow.

03

Motion FX & Color Grade

Integrating 3D Blender elements, kinetic subtitles, DaVinci Resolve color grading, and rich sound design.

04

4K Master Delivery

Final color calibrated render export formatted specifically for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, or YouTube algorithm specs.

Everything you need to know about working with Rakesh.EDIT.
